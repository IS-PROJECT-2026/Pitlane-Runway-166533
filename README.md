# Pit Lane & Runway

> How aviation and motorsport actually work — one system at a time.

## About

Pit Lane & Runway is an educational website that explains how aviation and Formula 1 systems work in a clear and accessible way.

The project focuses on taking technical concepts that can seem complicated and explaining them in a way that is understandable without requiring an engineering background.

## Domains

### Aviation

Topics explore the systems and processes behind aviation, including how aircraft operate and how aviation systems are coordinated.

### Formula 1

Topics explore the engineering, strategy, and systems behind Formula 1 racing.
